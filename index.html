We bring interesting ideas to life.

Puso Studios is a creative technology studio building thoughtful digital products, playful experiments and useful things for the web and mobile.

From an early idea scribbled in a notebook to a product people can actually use, we like figuring out how to make things real.

Ideas are easy. Making them real is the fun part.

Puso Studios is an independent software and product studio based in Estonia.

We work at the intersection of technology, design and curiosity — exploring ideas, solving interesting problems and turning concepts into well-crafted digital experiences.

Sometimes that means building a complete product from scratch. Sometimes it means experimenting with a strange little idea just to see where it goes.
